Agentify is a premium 24/7 AI sales assistant builder for modern websites.
It empowers SaaS platforms and business owners to deploy customized AI agents that capture leads, answer product questions, and provide conversation intelligence.
Agentify supports integrating manual text, FAQs, scraped websites, and uploaded business documents (PDF, DOCX, TXT) to build a robust retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) knowledge engine.
The platform features tier-based resource constraints, automated usage limit enforcement, and beautiful real-time analytics dashboards showing lead conversion rates, popular inquiries, and unanswered questions.
Agentify was developed using Next.js 16, Supabase, TailwindCSS, and Vertex AI models.
