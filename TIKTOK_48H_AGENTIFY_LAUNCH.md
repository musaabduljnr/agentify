# Agentify TikTok 48-Hour Launch Sprint

Goal: reach the first 1,000 relevant followers as fast as possible by posting useful, proof-led videos about Agentify: a SaaS that turns a website into a 24/7 AI sales assistant trained on the business's own content.

Important: 1,000 followers in 48 hours is not guaranteed. The controllable goal is to publish enough sharp tests that one or two videos can break out.

## Creative Rules

Use these rules for every post:
- Hook in the first 1-2 seconds.
- Show the product on-screen by second 3.
- Keep most videos 12-30 seconds.
- Add large on-screen captions.
- End with one action: follow, comment "AI", or try the free trial.
- Reply to every comment in the first hour.
- Turn good comments into reply videos.

This aligns with TikTok's own creative guidance to prioritize the hook early and use Creative Center/Creative Insights for patterns and inspiration.

## Fast Production Workflow

Record in batches:
1. Record six screen recordings first.
2. Record six face-to-camera intros.
3. Edit all in CapCut/TikTok editor.
4. Use the same visual pattern: hook text, product proof, one CTA.
5. Save hooks that perform; delete weak phrasing quickly.

Minimum asset list:
- Screen recording of landing page
- Screen recording of onboarding/training
- Screen recording of the chat widget
- Screen recording of lead capture
- Screen recording of analytics/dashboard
- Founder face-to-camera clip

## Do Not Do

- Do not buy followers.
- Do not use engagement pods.
- Do not post vague AI hype.
- Do not hide the product until the end.
- Do not send every viewer to a sales pitch. Teach, show, and invite.
